About PharmaChemE

PharmaChemE makes tools for chemical and pharmaceutical engineering: calculators, troubleshooting practice, a molecule tool and articles, built around the calculations that come up in day-to-day process and production work.

It covers process, pharmaceutical and chemical engineering calculations, troubleshooting, chemistry and digital engineering tools. The aim is to take calculations that usually sit buried in spreadsheets or textbooks and make them quick to use and easy to trust.

PharmaChemE also publishes chemical engineering videos on YouTube. You can also walk through a 3D Virtual Plant Tour of a chemical plant.

Who's Behind It

PharmaChemE brings plant experience and AI-assisted development together. It is directed, reviewed and maintained by Ankush Chopra, a chemical engineer (Thapar University).
