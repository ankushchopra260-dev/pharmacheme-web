About PharmaChemE

PharmaChemE is focused on practical tools for chemical and pharmaceutical engineering — calculators, troubleshooting practice, molecule tools, and engineering knowledge built around the calculations that come up in day-to-day process and production work.

The scope spans process engineering, pharmaceutical engineering, chemical engineering, engineering calculations, troubleshooting, chemistry, and digital engineering tools — with the goal of making calculations that are often buried in spreadsheets or textbooks quick to use and easy to trust.

PharmaChemE also publishes chemical engineering content on YouTube. You can also explore a 3D Virtual Plant Tour of a chemical plant.

Who's Behind It
