GATE CH formula sheets

The key equations for each section of the GATE Chemical Engineering syllabus, with the traps that cost marks. Revise one sheet, then practise that section.

OverviewFormula sheetsPractice questionsMock testTest series (Paid)
Engineering Mathematics13 marks (fixed). Thirteen marks are guaranteed from this section in every CH paper, and the questions are usually short.Process Calculationsabout 5 to 8 marks. Material and energy balances, recycle, bypass and purge, and the phase rule.Thermodynamicsabout 6 to 9 marks. Laws, property relations, equations of state, mixtures and phase and reaction equilibrium.Fluid Mechanics and Mechanical Operationsabout 9 to 12 marks. Pipe flow, pumps, flow meters, settling, fluidisation, size reduction and filtration.Heat Transferabout 7 to 10 marks. Conduction, convection, radiation, boiling and condensation, heat exchangers and evaporators.Mass Transferabout 9 to 12 marks. Diffusion, mass transfer coefficients, stage-wise and continuous contacting, and the main separation operations.Chemical Reaction Engineeringabout 9 to 12 marks. Kinetics, ideal reactors, multiple reactions, non-isothermal and non-ideal reactors, and catalysis.Instrumentation and Process Controlabout 7 to 9 marks. Measurement, dynamic response, transfer functions, controllers, stability and tuning.Plant Design and Economicsabout 4 to 6 marks. Cost estimation, depreciation, profitability and the time value of money, optimisation and batch scheduling.Chemical Technologyabout 3 to 6 marks. Process flow, raw materials, catalysts and conditions for the named inorganic, fertiliser, natural-product, petroleum and polymer processes.
