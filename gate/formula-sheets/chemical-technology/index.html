Chemical Technology: GATE CH formula sheet

Process flow, raw materials, catalysts and conditions for the named inorganic, fertiliser, natural-product, petroleum and polymer processes. Weight in the paper: about 3 to 6 marks.

OverviewFormula sheetsPractice questionsMock test

Inorganic chemicals

Synthesis gas
Steam reforming of methane over Ni, about 800–900 °C: CH4 + H2O → CO + 3H2Water-gas shift: Fe–Cr (high T), Cu–Zn (low T)
Sulfuric acid (contact process)
S → SO2 → SO3 over V2O5 (≈ 420–450 °C, exothermic) → absorbed in 98% acidDouble contact double absorption for > 99.7% conversion
Phosphoric acid (wet process)
Rock phosphate + H2SO4 → H3PO4 + gypsum
Chlor-alkali (membrane cell)
Anode: Cl2; cathode: H2 + NaOHMembrane passes Na+ only

Fertilisers

Ammonia
N2 + 3H2 ⇌ 2NH3, promoted Fe, 150–300 bar, 400–500 °CExothermic; low single-pass conversion, recycle loop with purge
Urea
2NH3 + CO2 → ammonium carbamate → urea + H2OCarbamate step exothermic, dehydration endothermic

Natural products

Kraft pulping
White liquor NaOH + Na2S; black liquor burnt in recovery boiler
Sugar and ethanol
Cane juice → clarification → evaporation → crystallisation; molasses → fermentation (yeast) → distillation95.6% ethanol–water azeotrope

Petroleum and polymers

Refining
Atmospheric and vacuum distillation, FCC (zeolite), hydrotreating, reforming (Pt)
Ethylbenzene → styrene
Benzene + ethylene (zeolite) → EB; dehydrogenation over Fe oxide with steam, endothermic
Ethylene oxide
Ethylene + O2 over Ag
Polyethylene and polyester
LDPE: high-pressure free radical; HDPE: Ziegler–Natta or Phillips; PET: terephthalic acid + ethylene glycol
Common traps
  • Know the catalyst and whether each key reaction is exo- or endothermic.
  • Know which steps run under high pressure (ammonia, urea, LDPE).
