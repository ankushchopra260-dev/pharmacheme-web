Mass Transfer: GATE CH formula sheet

Diffusion, mass transfer coefficients, stage-wise and continuous contacting, and the main separation operations. Weight in the paper: about 9 to 12 marks.

OverviewFormula sheetsPractice questionsMock test

Diffusion and coefficients

Fick's first law
JA = −DAB dCA/dz
Diffusion through stagnant B
NA = (DABP/RTz)(pA1 − pA2)/pBMEquimolar counter-diffusion: drop pBM
Theories
Film: k ∝ D; penetration and surface renewal: k ∝ D0.5
Chilton–Colburn analogy
jH = jD = f/2jD = Sh/(Re Sc1/3)

Distillation

Relative volatility
y = αx/(1 + (α − 1)x)
Fenske (minimum stages)
Nmin = ln[(xD/(1 − xD))((1 − xB)/xB)]/ln αAt total reflux, including the reboiler
Minimum reflux
Rmin = (xD − y*)/(y* − x*) at the pinchSaturated liquid feed: pinch at x = xF
q-line
y = qx/(q − 1) − xF/(q − 1)q = 1 saturated liquid (vertical), q = 0 saturated vapour (horizontal)
Operating line (rectifying)
y = (R/(R + 1))x + xD/(R + 1)

Absorption and extraction

Absorption factor
A = L/(mG)
Packed height
Z = HOG × NOGDilute, y* ≈ 0: NOG = ln(y1/y2)
Kremser (stages)
N = ln[((y1 − mx0)/(y2 − mx0))(1 − 1/A) + 1/A]/ln A
Single-stage extraction
Fraction extracted = E/(1 + E); E = KS/F

Drying and humidification

Drying periods
Constant rate: surface evaporation; falling rate: internal diffusion
Constant-rate time
tc = (Ws/A)(X1 − Xc)/Rc
Humidity
H = 0.622 pw/(P − pw)Wet bulb ≈ adiabatic saturation temperature for air–water
Common traps
  • Fenske counts the reboiler as a stage; subtract one for trays only.
  • Mole fractions vs mole ratios in absorption with high solute concentration.
  • Film theory gives k ∝ D, penetration theory k ∝ √D.
