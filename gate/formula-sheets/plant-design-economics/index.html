Plant Design and Economics: GATE CH formula sheet

Cost estimation, depreciation, profitability and the time value of money, optimisation and batch scheduling. Weight in the paper: about 4 to 6 marks.

Costs

Six-tenths rule
C2 = C1(Q2/Q1)0.6
Cost index
Cnow = Cthen × Inow/IthenCEPCI, Marshall & Swift
Lang factor
Fixed capital ≈ Lang factor × delivered equipment costAbout 3.1 solids, 3.6 solid–fluid, 4.7 fluid plants

Depreciation

Straight line
d = (V − Vs)/n
Declining balance
Book value = V(1 − f)n
Sum-of-years digits
dj = (n − j + 1)/(n(n + 1)/2) × (V − Vs)

Time value of money

Compound interest
F = P(1 + i)n; P = F/(1 + i)n
Capital recovery
A = P i(1 + i)n/((1 + i)n − 1)
Payback period
Fixed capital / annual cash flow (profit after tax + depreciation)
Rate of return and DCF
DCFRR: interest rate at which NPV = 0

Optimisation and scheduling

Optimum pipe diameter
Minimise pumping cost + annualised pipe cost
Optimum insulation
Minimise heat-loss cost + annualised insulation cost
Batch scheduling
Overlapping batches: cycle time set by the longest step (bottleneck)Makespan ≈ sum of first batch steps + (n − 1) × bottleneck
Common traps
  • Depreciation is a non-cash expense: add it back for cash flow.
  • Interest compounded more often than yearly: ieff = (1 + r/m)m − 1.
