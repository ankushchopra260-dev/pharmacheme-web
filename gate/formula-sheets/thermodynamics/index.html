Thermodynamics: GATE CH formula sheet

Laws, property relations, equations of state, mixtures and phase and reaction equilibrium. Weight in the paper: about 6 to 9 marks.

First and second law

Closed system
ΔU = Q − W
Open system, steady
ΔH + ΔKE + ΔPE = Q − Ws
Ideal gas processes
Isothermal: W = nRT ln(V2/V1); adiabatic reversible: PVγ = const
Entropy of ideal gas
ΔS = nCp ln(T2/T1) − nR ln(P2/P1)
Carnot
η = 1 − TC/TH; COPrefrigerator = TC/(TH − TC)Absolute temperatures

Property relations

Maxwell (from G)
(∂S/∂P)T = −(∂V/∂T)P
Clausius–Clapeyron
ln(P2/P1) = −(ΔHvap/R)(1/T2 − 1/T1)
Van der Waals
(P + a/V²)(V − b) = RTResidual property = real − ideal gas value at same T, P

Mixtures

Partial molar property
M̄i = (∂(nM)/∂ni)T,P,nj
Gibbs–Duhem
Σ xi dM̄i = 0 at constant T, P
Fugacity
fi = φi yi P (vapour); fi = γi xi fi° (liquid)Ideal gas: φ = 1
Margules (one parameter)
GE/RT = A x1x2; ln γ1 = A x2²

Phase and reaction equilibrium

Raoult's law
yiP = xiPisatModified: yiP = γixiPisat
Bubble and dew
Bubble P = Σ xiPisat; dew 1/P = Σ yi/Pisat
Equilibrium constant
ΔG° = −RT ln KVan 't Hoff: d ln K/dT = ΔH°/RT²
Common traps
  • Use kelvin in every thermodynamic formula.
  • Cp − Cv = R only for ideal gases.
  • A catalyst does not change K.
