Heat Transfer: GATE CH formula sheet

Conduction, convection, radiation, boiling and condensation, heat exchangers and evaporators. Weight in the paper: about 7 to 10 marks.

OverviewFormula sheetsPractice questionsMock test

Conduction

Plane wall
q = kAΔT/L; R = L/kA
Cylinder
R = ln(r2/r1)/(2πkL)
Critical radius
Cylinder: rc = k/h; sphere: rc = 2k/h
Lumped capacitance
Valid when Bi = hLc/k < 0.1; T − T∞ ∝ e−hAt/ρVcp

Convection

Nusselt number
Nu = hL/k
Dittus–Boelter
Nu = 0.023 Re0.8 Prnn = 0.4 heating, 0.3 cooling
Laminar tube, fully developed
Nu = 3.66 (constant wall T); 4.36 (constant flux)
Prandtl number
Pr = cpμ/k = ν/α

Radiation

Stefan–Boltzmann
E = εσT⁴; σ = 5.67 × 10−8 W/m²K⁴
Two grey surfaces
q = σ(T1⁴ − T2⁴)/[(1 − ε1)/ε1A1 + 1/A1F12 + (1 − ε2)/ε2A2]

Exchangers and evaporators

Overall coefficient
1/U = 1/hi + Rw + Rf + 1/ho (thin wall)
LMTD
ΔTlm = (ΔT1 − ΔT2)/ln(ΔT1/ΔT2); Q = UA F ΔTlmCounter-current gives the larger LMTD
Effectiveness–NTU
ε = Q/Qmax; NTU = UA/Cmin
Steam economy
kg water evaporated per kg steam ≈ number of effects (ideal)Capacity of an MEE ≈ that of one effect with the same total ΔT
Common traps
  • Use the LMTD correction factor F for multi-pass exchangers.
  • Adding insulation below the critical radius increases heat loss.
  • Radiation needs absolute temperatures.
