Dynamics

First order
G = K/(τs + 1); step: y = KM(1 − e−t/τ)63.2% at t = τ; 98% at 4τ
Second order
G = K/(τ²s² + 2ζτs + 1)ζ < 1 underdamped
Overshoot and decay ratio
OS = exp(−πζ/√(1 − ζ²)); decay ratio = OS²Period = 2πτ/√(1 − ζ²)
Dead time
G = e−θs; Padé: e−θs ≈ (1 − θs/2)/(1 + θs/2)

Common traps
  • Time constants and gains multiply in series only without interaction.
  • A pure integrator has no steady-state gain.
  • Inverse response comes from a right-half-plane zero.
