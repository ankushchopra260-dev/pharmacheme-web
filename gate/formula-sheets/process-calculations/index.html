Process Calculations: GATE CH formula sheet

Material and energy balances, recycle, bypass and purge, and the phase rule. These marks are often the easiest in the paper if you draw the block diagram first. Weight in the paper: about 5 to 8 marks.

OverviewFormula sheetsPractice questionsMock test

Balances

General balance
Accumulation = In − Out + Generation − ConsumptionSteady state: accumulation = 0
Degrees of freedom
DOF = unknowns − independent equationsSolve when DOF = 0
Tie component
Use a species that passes unchanged between streams to link themInerts, N2 in combustion, dry air in drying

Recycle, bypass and purge

Single-pass vs overall conversion
Xsp = (reactor in − reactor out)/reactor in; Xoverall = (fresh in − process out)/fresh in
Purge for inerts
Inert in fresh feed = purge flow × inert fraction in recyclePurge composition = recycle composition

Combustion

Theoretical oxygen
For complete combustion of all C to CO2 and H to H2OExcess air % = (O2 fed − theoretical)/theoretical × 100
Air
21 mol% O2, 79 mol% N2: N2/O2 = 3.76Orsat (dry) analysis excludes H2O

Energy and phase rule

Sensible heat
Q = m ∫cp dT
Heat of reaction
ΔHr = Σ ν ΔHf(products) − Σ ν ΔHf(reactants)Hess law
Gibbs phase rule
F = C − P + 2 (non-reacting); F = C − P + 2 − r (with r independent reactions)Triple point of a pure substance: F = 0
Humidity
H = 0.622 pw/(P − pw) kg/kg dry airRelative humidity = pw/psat
Common traps
  • Dry-basis flue gas: leave water out of the total.
  • Use moles, not mass, for reaction balances.
  • Excess air is based on the oxygen needed for complete combustion, even if combustion is incomplete.
