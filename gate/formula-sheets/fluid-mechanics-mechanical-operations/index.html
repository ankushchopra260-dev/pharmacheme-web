Fluid Mechanics and Mechanical Operations: GATE CH formula sheet

Pipe flow, pumps, flow meters, settling, fluidisation, size reduction and filtration. Weight in the paper: about 9 to 12 marks.

Pipe flow

Reynolds number
Re = ρvD/μLaminar < 2100 in pipes
Hagen–Poiseuille
ΔP = 32μLv/D²Laminar; vmax = 2vavg
Friction factor
Fanning f = 16/Re (laminar); Darcy fD = 4fBlasius: f = 0.079 Re−0.25 (turbulent, smooth)
Head loss
hf = 4f (L/D)(v²/2g)
Bernoulli
P/ρg + v²/2g + z = constantTorricelli: v = √(2gh)

Pumps and meters

Pump power
P = ρgQH/η
NPSH available
NPSHA = (Ps − Pv)/ρg + v²/2gMust exceed NPSHR
Affinity laws
Q ∝ N; H ∝ N²; P ∝ N³
Orifice and venturi
Q = Cd A0 √(2ΔP/ρ(1 − β⁴))Cd ≈ 0.61 orifice, ≈ 0.98 venturi

Particles and beds

Stokes' law
vt = g d²(ρp − ρ)/18μRep < 1
Ergun
ΔP/L = 150μv(1 − ε)²/(ε³d²) + 1.75ρv²(1 − ε)/(ε³d)Kozeny–Carman is the first (laminar) term
Fluidisation
ΔP = (1 − ε)(ρp − ρ)gLBed weight per area, constant above minimum fluidisation
Size reduction
Rittinger: E ∝ (1/D2 − 1/D1); Kick: E ∝ ln(D1/D2); Bond: E ∝ (1/√D2 − 1/√D1)Rittinger for fine, Kick for coarse grinding
Constant-pressure filtration
t/V = (μαc/2A²ΔP)V + μRm/(AΔP)Slope → cake resistance; intercept → medium resistance
Common traps
  • Fanning vs Darcy friction factor differ by a factor of 4.
  • Check Rep before using Stokes' law.
  • Pump power uses total head, not just the static lift.
