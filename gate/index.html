Exam-day strategy

  • Do the NATs and MSQs you can solve first. They carry no negative marks, so a careful attempt never costs you.
  • Guess MCQs only when you can rule out two options. With four options and a 1/3 penalty, a blind guess has an expected value of zero.
  • Practise on the virtual calculator. It is slower than a handheld one; our mock test and practice questions use a calculator with the same layout and keys as the one in the exam.
  • Read the rounding instruction in every NAT. Keep full precision until the last step.
  • Bank General Aptitude. Fifteen marks for an hour of practice a week is the best return in the paper.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GATE Chemical Engineering exam pattern?

GATE CH is a 3-hour computer-based test with 65 questions for 100 marks: 10 General Aptitude questions (15 marks) and 55 Chemical Engineering questions (85 marks, of which 13 marks are Engineering Mathematics). Questions are MCQ, MSQ or NAT, worth 1 or 2 marks.

Is there negative marking in GATE?

Only for MCQs: a wrong answer costs 1/3 mark on a 1-mark question and 2/3 mark on a 2-mark question. MSQ and NAT questions have no negative marking, and MSQs give no partial credit.

Which institute conducts GATE 2027?

IIT Madras is the organising institute for GATE 2027. Dates, registration and the information brochure are on the official site, gate2027.iitm.ac.in.

What is new in the GATE 2027 CH syllabus?

The syllabus has been revised for 2027. Engineering Mathematics now includes fundamentals of AI and machine learning: linear regression and principal component analysis. Check the official syllabus PDF for the full list.
