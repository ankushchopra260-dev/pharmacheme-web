Boilers Act, 2025: Compliance for Plants

Statutory Compliance · Utilities · October 2026

Almost every chemical plant has a boiler, and a boiler holds a great deal of stored energy. India replaced the century-old Indian Boilers Act, 1923 with the Boilers Act, 2025, in force from 1 May 2025. The basic duties are familiar: register, inspect, operate with certified people and report accidents. It is a general guide, not legal advice; the Act, the rules, your state’s notifications and your own licence conditions always prevail.

What changed Basics

The Boilers Act, 2025 modernises the 1923 law. A Central Boilers Board regulates design, manufacture, erection and use, aiming for uniform registration and inspection across states. Several minor offences have been decriminalised, with fines converted into penalties decided by adjudication. Rules, regulations and orders made under the old Act continue until replaced, so existing procedures carry on while new ones are notified.

Registration and inspection Basics

A boiler covered by the Act must be registered before use and may be used only with a valid certificate after inspection by the inspecting authority, with periodic inspections thereafter. Steam pipes and fittings covered by the Act are included. Any repair or alteration of the pressure parts needs the authority’s approval, using a certified boiler repairer.

Certified persons Working level

Boilers must be operated under the charge of persons holding the required certificate of proficiency or competency, with qualifications prescribed by the government, and repairs done by certified repairers. Shift planning should make sure every shift has a certified attendant; see HR in plants.

Accidents Working level

Boiler accidents must be reported to the inspecting authority, and an inquiry is held in the manner the state prescribes. Leave the scene undisturbed except for rescue and making safe.

Good boiler house practice Going deeper

  • Test safety valves, low-water cut-offs and flame failure trips on a schedule and record them.
  • Control feed water quality and blowdown to prevent scale and corrosion; see steam system efficiency.
  • Keep the boiler log, the inspection certificate and repair records in the boiler house.
  • Never bypass a trip to keep the plant running.
On the plant

Treat the boiler certificate expiry date like a licence: put it on the compliance register with a reminder well ahead, because the inspection needs a planned shutdown and steam for the plant during it.

Mistakes I see most often

  • Running on an expired certificate.
  • Repairs to pressure parts by uncertified welders.
  • No certified attendant on night shift.
  • Bypassed low-water trips.
  • Poor water treatment causing tube failures.

Questions people ask

When did the Boilers Act, 2025 come into force?

On 1 May 2025, replacing the Indian Boilers Act, 1923.

Do rules under the old Boilers Act still apply?

Rules, regulations and orders under the 1923 Act continue until new ones are notified under the 2025 Act.

Who may repair a boiler?

A certified boiler repairer, with approval of the inspecting authority for repairs to pressure parts.
