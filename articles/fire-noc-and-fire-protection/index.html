Fire NOC and Fire Protection for Chemical Plants

Statutory Compliance · Fire Safety · October 2026

A solvent fire in a production block can spread in minutes. The fire NOC from the state fire service confirms a building has the fire protection its occupancy needs; keeping that protection working every day is the site’s job. It is a general guide, not legal advice; the Act, the rules, your state’s notifications and your own licence conditions always prevail.

The fire NOC Basics

Fire safety is regulated by each state under its fire services law, generally referring to the National Building Code of India (Part 4, Fire and Life Safety). Industrial buildings with hazardous occupancy need a fire NOC from the state fire service, first at the building plan stage and then before occupation, with renewal at the interval the state sets. Any change of building use, layout or hazard level needs an amended NOC.

Protection systems for a chemical plant Basics

  • Hydrant system: fire water tank, jockey, main electric and diesel pumps, ring main and hydrants with hoses.
  • Sprinklers and water spray for buildings and for cooling tanks and structures.
  • Foam systems for solvent tank farms and drum stores; water alone is poor on many solvent fires.
  • Fire detection and alarm: smoke and heat detectors, flame detectors in solvent areas, gas detectors for flammable vapours.
  • Extinguishers of the right type (foam, CO₂, dry powder) at each location.

Prevention comes first Working level

Most plant fires start from solvent vapour meeting an ignition source. Prevention: earthing and bonding, inerting, flameproof electrics, hot work under permit, good housekeeping, and keeping solvent stocks in production areas to the shift requirement.

Testing and maintenance Working level

  • Run fire pumps weekly, including the diesel pump, and check auto-start on pressure drop.
  • Test hydrants, sprinklers, detectors and alarms on a schedule and record the results.
  • Keep the fire water tank full and its level indicated.
  • Inspect and refill extinguishers as required.

Fire teams and drills Going deeper

Train a fire team on every shift, with clear roles in the on-site emergency plan, and practise with real equipment. Agree in advance how the state fire service will reach the site, where it will connect, and what information it will need on arrival, including a list of chemicals and their locations.

On the plant

Check the diesel fire pump at night. A pump that starts only when the day-shift fitter is there to prime it is not a fire pump.

Mistakes I see most often

  • Fire NOC not amended after a layout change.
  • Water used where foam is needed.
  • Diesel fire pump never test-run on auto-start.
  • Hydrants blocked by stored material.
  • Fire team only on the day shift.

Questions people ask

Who issues a fire NOC?

The state fire service, under the state’s fire services law, usually based on the National Building Code of India.

Why is foam used for solvent fires?

Foam forms a blanket on the liquid surface that cuts off oxygen and vapour; water can spread a burning solvent that floats on it.

How often should fire pumps be tested?

A weekly run, including the diesel pump and an automatic start test, is common good practice.
