Potassium tert-butoxide (KOtBu): properties and safe handling

CAS 865-47-4. Also called KOtBu, potassium t-butoxide, KTB. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Melting / freezing pointabout 256 °C (decomposes)

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Charge under nitrogen; do not leave opened containers in humid air.
  • Can react violently with DMSO, DMF and chlorinated solvents at higher temperature: test first.
