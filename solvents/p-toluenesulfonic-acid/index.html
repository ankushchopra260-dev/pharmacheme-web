p-Toluenesulfonic acid (PTSA, monohydrate): properties and safe handling

CAS 6192-52-5. Also called PTSA, pTsOH, tosic acid. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

p-Toluenesulfonic acid properties

Identity and physical
FormulaCH3C6H4SO3H·H2O
CAS number6192-52-5
Molecular weight190.22 g/mol
Density1.24 g/cm³ (solid)
Melting / freezing point103 to 106 °C (monohydrate)
Boiling point140 °C at 20 mmHg (anhydrous)
With waterVery soluble (about 67 g per 100 mL)

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • With alcohols forms alkyl tosylates, which are genotoxic impurities.
