Sodium hydroxide (caustic soda): properties and safe handling

CAS 1310-73-2. Also called NaOH, caustic soda, lye, caustic lye. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • 50 % lye freezes at about 12 °C: heat-trace lines and tanks in winter.
  • Hot strong caustic attacks glass lining and causes caustic cracking of carbon steel above about 60 °C.
