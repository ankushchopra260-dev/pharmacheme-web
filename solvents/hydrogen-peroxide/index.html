Hydrogen peroxide (30 to 50 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 7722-84-1. Also called H2O2, peroxide. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Vented containers only: decomposition gas can burst a sealed drum.
  • Never return unused peroxide to the stock drum.
  • With acetone and acid forms explosive peroxides; with acetic or formic acid forms peracids.
