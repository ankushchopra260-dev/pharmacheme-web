Sodium methoxide (25 to 30 % in methanol): properties and safe handling

CAS 124-41-4. Also called NaOMe, sodium methylate, SMO. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Solid sodium methoxide is a self-heating dust: charge under nitrogen and keep away from moisture.
  • Pump the solution with earthed equipment: methanol vapour is flammable.
