Liquor ammonia (aqueous ammonia, 25 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 1336-21-6. Also called aqueous ammonia, ammonium hydroxide, ammonia solution, NH4OH. This is a solution, mixture or solid rather than a pure solvent, so there is no vacuum boiling-point table: the properties below are for the usual commercial strength.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Drums build up pressure in summer: cool before opening and open slowly, facing away.
  • Do not use copper, brass or zinc: ammonia attacks them. Use carbon or stainless steel.
  • Vent to a water or acid scrubber; ammonia is lighter than air but cold vapour can stay low.
