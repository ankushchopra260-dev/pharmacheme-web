Sodium azide: properties and safe handling

CAS 26628-22-8. Also called NaN3, azide. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • With acids forms hydrazoic acid (HN3): very toxic and explosive.
  • With DCM or chloroform forms explosive di- and triazidomethane: never use halogenated solvents with azide.
  • Forms explosive heavy-metal azides with copper, lead, brass and solder: no such metals in contact, and never pour into drains.
