Sodium metal: properties and safe handling

CAS 7440-23-5. Also called Na, sodium. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Store under dry mineral oil or paraffin.
  • Can explode with chlorinated solvents (CCl4, chloroform, DCM).
  • Destroy residues with isopropanol or tert-butanol before water.
