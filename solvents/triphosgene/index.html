Triphosgene: properties and safe handling

CAS 32315-10-9. Also called bis(trichloromethyl) carbonate, BTC. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Triphosgene properties

Identity and physical
FormulaC3Cl6O3
CAS number32315-10-9
Molecular weight296.75 g/mol
Densityabout 1.78 g/cm³ (solid)
Melting / freezing point79 to 83 °C
Boiling pointabout 205 °C (decomposes, giving phosgene)
With waterSlowly hydrolyses with water and moisture, giving phosgene, HCl and CO2

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Treat as phosgene: closed handling, phosgene detection badges or monitors, caustic or ammonia scrubber.
  • Phosgene smell (fresh hay) appears only above the exposure limit.
