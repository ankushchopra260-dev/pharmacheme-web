Hydrochloric acid (HCl, 30 to 37 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 7647-01-0. Also called HCl, muriatic acid, hydrogen chloride solution. This is a solution, mixture or solid rather than a pure solvent, so there is no vacuum boiling-point table: the properties below are for the usual commercial strength.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Strong acid fumes: vent storage tanks and reactors to a water or caustic scrubber.
  • Never mix with sodium hypochlorite (bleach): releases chlorine gas.
  • Use rubber-lined, FRP, PP or glass-lined equipment: it attacks stainless steel quickly.
  • Excel sheets often list the azeotrope boiling point (108.5 °C) as if it were the boiling point of commercial acid. Concentrated acid starts losing HCl gas well below that.
