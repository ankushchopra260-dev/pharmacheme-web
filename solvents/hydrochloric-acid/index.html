Hydrochloric acid (HCl, 30 to 37 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 7647-01-0. Also called HCl, muriatic acid, hydrogen chloride solution. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Strong acid fumes: vent storage tanks and reactors to a water or caustic scrubber.
  • Never mix with sodium hypochlorite (bleach): releases chlorine gas.
  • Use rubber-lined, FRP, PP or glass-lined equipment: it attacks stainless steel quickly.
  • Excel sheets often list the azeotrope boiling point (108.5 °C) as if it were the boiling point of commercial acid. Concentrated acid starts losing HCl gas well below that.
