Petroleum ether (light petroleum, 60 to 80 °C): properties and safe handling

CAS 8032-32-4. Also called petroleum spirit, light petroleum, pet ether, ligroin, benzine. This is a solution, mixture or solid rather than a pure solvent, so there is no vacuum boiling-point table: the properties below are for the usual commercial strength.

Petroleum ether properties

Identity and physical
FormulaMixture of C5 to C7 alkanes
CAS number8032-32-4
Molecular weightabout 80 to 90 (average) g/mol
Densityabout 0.64 g/mL (40 to 60 grade) to 0.67 g/mL (60 to 80 grade) at 20 °C
Melting / freezing pointbelow −70 °C
Boiling pointA boiling range, set by the grade: 40 to 60, 60 to 80 or 80 to 100 °C
Specific heat (liquid)about 0.53 kcal/kg·°C (2.2 kJ/kg·K)
Latent heat of vaporisationabout 80 kcal/kg (335 kJ/kg)
Viscosityabout 0.3 cP at 25 °C
Vapour density (air = 1)about 3
With waterNot miscible (forms two layers)
Mixing hazardsCheck what it reacts with: acids, bases, oxidisers, acid chlorides, water →
Material compatibilityRatings for 20 materials: SS316, MS, Hastelloy, glass-lined, PTFE, PP, PVC and gasket rubbers →
Fire and reactivity
Flash pointabout -40 °C (closed cup)
Fire and reactivityFlash point below −20 °C (about −40 °C for 40 to 60, −25 °C for 60 to 80 grade); static accumulator
Autoignition temperatureabout 250 to 290 °C
Flammable limits (LEL to UEL)about 1 to 7 % by volume
Health and regulatory
Exposure limit (TLV)Depends on n-hexane content (n-hexane TWA 50 ppm); 300 ppm TWA for low-hexane grades
Main health effectsDrowsiness; nerve damage if rich in n-hexane; aspiration hazard if swallowed
Cancer (IARC)Not listed

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Not an ether: the name is historical. It is a light hydrocarbon cut, close to hexane in behaviour.
  • Ask the supplier for the n-hexane content: it sets the exposure limit and the health risk.
  • Static accumulator: earth and bond, inert the vessel, limit pumping velocity.
