Sulphuric acid (H2SO4, 98 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 7664-93-9. Also called sulfuric acid, H2SO4, oil of vitriol. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • 98 % acid can freeze in winter (about +3 °C): heat-trace lines and keep tanks above 10 °C.
  • Carbon steel is fine for 93 to 98 % acid at ambient temperature and low velocity; dilute acid needs alloy, PTFE or lined equipment.
  • Adding water to concentrated acid can boil it locally and throw acid out of the vessel.
