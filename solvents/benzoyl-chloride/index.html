Benzoyl chloride: properties and safe handling

CAS 98-88-4. Also called BzCl, benzenecarbonyl chloride. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Benzoyl chloride properties

FormulaC6H5COCl
CAS number98-88-4
Molecular weight140.57 g/mol
Density1.21 g/mL at 20 °C

Boiling point197 °C

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Lachrymator: handle in closed systems.
