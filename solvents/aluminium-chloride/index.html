Aluminium chloride (anhydrous): properties and safe handling

CAS 7446-70-0. Also called AlCl3, aluminum chloride. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Old drums can build pressure from moisture ingress (HCl): open slowly with ventilation.
  • Friedel–Crafts quenches are strongly exothermic with HCl release: reverse quench into ice/water with cooling.
