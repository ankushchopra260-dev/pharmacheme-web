Acetyl chloride: properties and safe handling

CAS 75-36-5. Also called ethanoyl chloride, AcCl. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Acetyl chloride properties

FormulaCH3COCl

Molecular weight78.50 g/mol
Density1.10 g/mL at 20 °C
Melting / freezing point−112 °C
Boiling point51 °C

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Both water-reactive and highly flammable: keep dry, inerted and earthed.
