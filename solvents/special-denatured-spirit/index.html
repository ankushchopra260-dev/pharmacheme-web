Special denatured spirit (SDS): properties and safe handling

CAS 64-17-5 (ethanol). Also called SDS, denatured alcohol, denatured spirit, rectified spirit (denatured). This is a solution, mixture or solid rather than a pure solvent, so there is no vacuum boiling-point table: the properties below are for the usual commercial strength.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • The denaturant and its amount are set by the formula under your state excise rules; check the licence and certificate of analysis.
  • In India, buying, storing and using SDS needs a licence under state excise rules; keep stock records.
  • For heat and vapour calculations, treat it as about 95 % ethanol.
