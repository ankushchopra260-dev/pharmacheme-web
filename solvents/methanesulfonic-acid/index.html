Methanesulfonic acid (MSA): properties and safe handling

CAS 75-75-2. Also called MSA, mesylic acid. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Density1.48 g/mL at 20 °C
Melting / freezing point20 °C (can freeze in cool weather)

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • With alcohols (methanol, ethanol, IPA) forms alkyl mesylates, which are genotoxic impurities: control temperature, time and residual alcohol.
