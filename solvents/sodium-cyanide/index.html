Sodium cyanide: properties and safe handling

CAS 143-33-9. Also called NaCN, cyanide. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Sodium cyanide properties

Identity and physical
FormulaNaCN
CAS number143-33-9
Molecular weight49.01 g/mol
Density1.6 g/cm³ (solid)
Melting / freezing point564 °C
Boiling point1,496 °C
With waterVery soluble (about 58 g per 100 mL at 20 °C); solutions give off HCN slowly, fast with acid

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Keep pH above 10.5 in cyanide solutions and waste to hold HCN in solution.
  • Have an approved cyanide antidote kit and trained first aiders on site; HCN monitors in the area.
