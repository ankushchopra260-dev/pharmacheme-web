Bromine: properties and safe handling

CAS 7726-95-6. Also called Br2. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Very dense: check pump and line design for the high density.
  • Keep sodium thiosulfate or bisulfite solution ready for spills.
