Sodium hydride (60 % in mineral oil): properties and safe handling

CAS 7646-69-7. Also called NaH. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • NaH in DMF, DMAc or DMSO has caused runaway decompositions and explosions, from about 40 to 50 °C.
  • Hydrogen release: vent to a safe place, keep the vessel inerted, and calculate the H2 rate for the vent.
  • Quench excess with isopropanol or ethanol before water, slowly and cold.
