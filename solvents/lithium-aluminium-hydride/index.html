Lithium aluminium hydride (LAH): properties and safe handling

CAS 16853-85-3. Also called LAH, LiAlH4, lithium tetrahydridoaluminate. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Use as a solution in THF where possible; avoid grinding the solid.
  • Quench by a defined sequence (for example ethyl acetate, then the Fieser water/NaOH steps), slowly with cooling and hydrogen venting.
