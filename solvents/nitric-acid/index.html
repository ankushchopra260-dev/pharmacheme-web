Nitric acid (HNO3, 60 to 98 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 7697-37-2. Also called HNO3, aqua fortis, fuming nitric acid. This is a solution, mixture or solid rather than a pure solvent, so there is no vacuum boiling-point table: the properties below are for the usual commercial strength.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Keep away from solvents, amines and reducing agents: nitrations and spills onto organics can run away.
  • Stainless steel 304L/316L suits most strengths; fuming acid needs aluminium or special alloys.
  • Anyone exposed to brown fumes should be watched for 24 hours: lung symptoms can appear late.
