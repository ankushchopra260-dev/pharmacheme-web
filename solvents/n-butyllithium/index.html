n-Butyllithium (in hexanes): properties and safe handling

CAS 109-72-8. Also called n-BuLi, BuLi, butyllithium. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Transfer only by pressure or closed pump systems under nitrogen; check lines are dry and oxygen-free.
  • Reacts with THF above about 0 °C: hold lithiations in THF cold.
