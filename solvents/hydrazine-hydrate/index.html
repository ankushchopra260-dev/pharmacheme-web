Hydrazine hydrate (80 to 100 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 7803-57-8. Also called N2H4·H2O, hydrazine monohydrate. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Keep away from rust, copper and oxidisers; nitrogen-blanket storage.
  • Hydrazine is mutagenic: control as a potential genotoxic impurity in the API.
