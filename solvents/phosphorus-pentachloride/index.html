Phosphorus pentachloride (PCl5): properties and safe handling

CAS 10026-13-8. Also called PCl5, phosphorus(V) chloride. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Plant handling notes

  • A solid, so it has no boiling point under vacuum: charge through a dry, nitrogen-purged solids addition system.
  • Every charge releases HCl: vent through a scrubber.
  • Keep containers tightly closed; moisture turns it into a fuming, pressurised mess.
