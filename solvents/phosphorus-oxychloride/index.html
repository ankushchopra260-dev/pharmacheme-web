Phosphorus oxychloride (POCl3): properties and safe handling

CAS 10025-87-3. Also called POCl3, phosphoryl chloride. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Quench incidents are common: unreacted POCl3 and phosphorodichloridic intermediates build up in a cold quench, then react all at once. Dose into the quench (reverse quench) with good stirring at a temperature where it reacts as it is added.
  • With DMF forms the Vilsmeier reagent; POCl3/DMF mixtures have runaway decomposition reported when held warm.
  • Freezes at about 1 °C: heat-trace lines in winter.
