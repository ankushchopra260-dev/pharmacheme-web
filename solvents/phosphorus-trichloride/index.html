Phosphorus trichloride (PCl3): properties and safe handling

CAS 7719-12-2. Also called PCl3, phosphorus(III) chloride. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Charge under dry nitrogen through closed lines; fumes on contact with air.
  • Quench slowly into a stirred, cooled quench and vent to a caustic scrubber sized for the HCl.
