Oxalyl chloride: properties and safe handling

CAS 79-37-8. Also called (COCl)2, ethanedioyl dichloride. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Gas evolution (CO, CO2, HCl) is large: monitor CO in the area and vent safely.
  • With DMSO (Swern) react only far below 0 °C; with DMF forms the Vilsmeier reagent and dimethylcarbamoyl chloride traces.
