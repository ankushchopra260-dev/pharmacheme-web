Sodium nitrite: properties and safe handling

CAS 7632-00-0. Also called NaNO2, nitrite. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • With amines or amide solvents (DMF, DMAc, NMP), especially in acid, forms N-nitrosamines (potent carcinogens). Keep nitrite quench streams segregated, and assess nitrosamine risk for any API step that uses nitrite.
  • Destroy excess nitrite with sulfamic acid or urea, with NOx scrubbing.
