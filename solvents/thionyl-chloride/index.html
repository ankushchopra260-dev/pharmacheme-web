Thionyl chloride (SOCl2): properties and safe handling

CAS 7719-09-7. Also called SOCl2, sulfinyl chloride, sulphur oxychloride. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Every mole used releases up to 2 moles of gas (SO2 + HCl): size the vent and scrubber for the peak gas rate.
  • With DMF (catalyst) forms the Vilsmeier reagent: keep the DMF charge small and the temperature controlled.
  • Distil off excess under vacuum before quench to cut the quench exotherm.
