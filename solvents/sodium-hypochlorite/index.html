Sodium hypochlorite (10 to 15 % bleach): properties and safe handling

CAS 7681-52-9. Also called NaOCl, bleach, hypo, javel water. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Never mix with acids (chlorine gas) or ammonia and amines (chloramines).
  • Loses strength on storage, faster when warm: check available chlorine before use.
