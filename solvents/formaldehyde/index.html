Formaldehyde and formalin (37 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 50-00-0. Also called formalin, methanal, formol, HCHO. This is a solution, mixture or solid rather than a pure solvent, so there is no vacuum boiling-point table: the properties below are for the usual commercial strength.

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Store formalin between about 15 and 35 °C: cold storage deposits paraformaldehyde solids; methanol is added as a stabiliser.
  • Odour threshold is about 0.5 to 1 ppm, above the exposure limit: do not rely on smell.
