Formaldehyde and formalin (37 %): properties and safe handling

CAS 50-00-0. Also called formalin, methanal, formol, HCHO. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Formaldehyde properties

Density1.08 to 1.09 g/mL (37 % formalin with 10 to 15 % methanol) at 20 °C; pure liquid 0.815 g/mL at −20 °C

Boiling point−19 °C (pure formaldehyde is a gas); formalin boils at about 96 to 98 °C

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Store formalin between about 15 and 35 °C: cold storage deposits paraformaldehyde solids; methanol is added as a stabiliser.
  • Odour threshold is about 0.5 to 1 ppm, above the exposure limit: do not rely on smell.
