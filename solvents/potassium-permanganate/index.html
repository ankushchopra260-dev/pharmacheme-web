Potassium permanganate: properties and safe handling

CAS 7722-64-7. Also called KMnO4, permanganate. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

Potassium permanganate properties

Identity and physical
FormulaKMnO4
CAS number7722-64-7
Molecular weight158.03 g/mol
Density2.70 g/cm³ (solid)
Melting / freezing pointdecomposes about 240 °C
Boiling point–
With waterSoluble (about 6.4 g per 100 mL at 20 °C)
Mixing hazardsCheck what it reacts with: acids, bases, oxidisers, acid chlorides, water →
Fire and reactivity
Flash pointnot flammable
Fire and reactivityNot flammable but a strong oxidiser: ignites glycerol, ethylene glycol and other organics; with concentrated sulphuric acid forms explosive Mn2O7
Autoignition temperature–
Flammable limits (LEL to UEL)–
Health and regulatory
Exposure limit (TLV)TWA 0.02 mg/m³ (respirable Mn)
Main health effectsHarmful if swallowed; stains and irritates skin
Cancer (IARC)Not listed
ICH Q3C residual solventNot a solvent (reagent)
GroupReactive reagents

Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Keep away from organic materials, acids and reducing agents.
