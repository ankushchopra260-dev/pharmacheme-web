Potassium permanganate: properties and safe handling

CAS 7722-64-7. Also called KMnO4, permanganate. Properties are for the usual commercial grade, with plant handling notes. Before mixing it with anything, run it through the mixing hazard checker.

FormulaKMnO4
CAS number7722-64-7
Molecular weight158.03 g/mol
Density2.70 g/cm³ (solid)
Melting / freezing pointdecomposes about 240 °C

Cancer (IARC)Not listed

NFPA 704 ratings are the published values; supplier ratings can differ, so use the one on your SDS. Typical values for commercial grades from handbook and supplier data; strength, stabiliser and supplier change the exact figures, so check the certificate of analysis and SDS. Exposure limits are ACGIH TLVs; check the current booklet and the Factories Act limits for your state.

Plant handling notes

  • Keep away from organic materials, acids and reducing agents.
