Plant Operator

Walk a compact pilot plant, observe equipment, and operate it directly — not a menu, an actual plant floor. This build (V3): the full charge-to-transfer batch sequence is playable on Reactor R-101 — charge it, bring it to temperature with the agitator and steam valve, hold it steady, cool it with the cooling water valve, and transfer it out.
