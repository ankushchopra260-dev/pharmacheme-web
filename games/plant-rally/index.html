Plant Rally

Drive the emergency vehicle round a chemical plant. Go, stop and change lane to stay safe, and answer an engineering question at each glowing unit. Every answer comes with its worked solution.

How to play

The car follows the road by itself. You decide when to go and stop, and which lane to drive in. A line at the top of the game always tells you what to do next.

  • Go and stop: hold Drive (↑ on a keyboard) to go, Brake (↓) to stop. In Student mode a speed limiter keeps you at the zone limit; in Professional mode you keep to it yourself.
  • Lanes: tap ◀ or ▶ (← or → on a keyboard) to move round a spill, a steam leak or a hot work barricade.
  • Stop and wait: at a gas alarm, stop before the red line until the all-clear. At a forklift crossing, give way.
  • Questions: two units light up on each lap. The car stops there by itself and asks one engineering question. You get 45 seconds in Student mode. A right answer takes 5 seconds off your time; a wrong one adds 10 seconds. After every question you see the full worked solution, and the race clock waits while you read it.
  • Safety: speeding, driving into a hazard or not giving way is a safety violation. Three violations end the race.

On the plant

The plant in the game is fictional. Your own site rules on vehicle speed, gas alarms and permit areas come first.

Questions

Why does a plant game punish speeding?

Vehicles in process areas and classified flammable zones must keep to low limits, and a vehicle is an ignition source. In Plant Rally the zone limits are the track limits: three violations end the race as an incident.

Are the mission answers correct?

Each mission is computed with the same formulas and data as the PharmaChemE calculator it links to, and every mission is checked against those calculators in automated tests before release.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The car follows the road by itself, so on a phone you only use four buttons: two to change lane, plus Drive and Brake.

Is my score saved?

Your best time, the ghost of your best lap and which missions you have solved are kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.
