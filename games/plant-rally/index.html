How to play

Drive around the circuit and stop at six mission bays. Your race clock keeps running while you answer. Correct answers improve your engineering score; incorrect answers show the working and cost time. Overspeeding, entering a simulated gas-alarm zone, or hitting a person/vehicle is a safety violation. Three violations end the shift.

Engineering missions

The prototype includes randomized practice questions on absolute pressure, pump power, LMTD, flash steam, line velocity and tank filling losses. Treat the questions as learning exercises, not as design approval. Check assumptions, units and applicable engineering standards before using any calculation for real equipment.

Safety first

Plant Rally rewards safe driving and sound judgement, not reckless speed. All locations and events are fictional. Emergency response in a real facility must follow the site emergency plan and approved procedures.
