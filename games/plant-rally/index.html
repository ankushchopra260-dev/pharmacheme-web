Plant Rally

Drive the emergency vehicle round a chemical plant against the clock. Speed limits are the track limits, hazards need the right response, and every stop is an engineering calculation.

How to play

  • Three laps, two missions a lap. Each lap lights up two units. Stop inside the yellow box at each one to open its mission. The race clock keeps running while you work, so a quick, correct answer is the fastest way round.
  • Correct answer: a boost for the next straight. In Professional mode it also opens the shortcut gate past the cooling towers for that lap.
  • Wrong answer or out of time: 10 seconds added. The working is saved for the review at the end, with a link to the calculator that solves it.
  • Speed limits: 40 km/h on plant roads, 20 km/h in process areas and 10 km/h in classified flammable zones. The kerb colour and the sign show the zone. Staying more than 2 km/h over the limit for 3 seconds is a violation.
  • Three violations end the race as an incident, with no finish time.

Controls. Laptop: arrow keys or WASD, Shift or B to boost, R to report a spill, P to pause, 1 to 4 to pick an answer. Phone: steer with ◀ ▶ on the left, Drive and Brake on the right.

Wrong answers in Student mode are the usual mistakes: gauge pressure used as absolute, a forgotten efficiency, weight % confused with mole %. Missions you get wrong come back more often in later races until you solve them.

On the plant

The plant in the game is fictional. Your own site rules on vehicle speed, gas alarms and permit areas come first.

Questions

Why does a plant game punish speeding?

Vehicles in process areas and classified flammable zones must keep to low limits, and a vehicle is an ignition source. In Plant Rally the zone limits are the track limits: three violations end the race as an incident.

Are the mission answers correct?

Each mission is computed with the same formulas and data as the PharmaChemE calculator it links to, and every mission is checked against those calculators in automated tests before release.

Is my score saved?

Your best time, the ghost of your best lap and which missions you have solved are kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.
