PharmaChemE Troubleshooter

Test your chemical and pharmaceutical plant troubleshooting skills against realistic operating scenarios.

How to Play

Click Start Shift to begin. Each round drops a live upset on the panel with no diagnosis given — a batch sheet and a short narrative are all you get. Click Go to Panel to see the live gauge and two control stations beside the vessel.

Click either control station to flip its state (e.g. a valve between OPEN/CLOSED, a cooling source between MAIN CW/CHILLED). The gauge reading updates immediately. As soon as the reading settles into the target range, the round locks in automatically — there's no separate "submit" button.

Scoring

Each round starts at 100 points. Every click beyond your first two costs 15 points, and passing through a genuinely dangerous configuration along the way costs 25 more (points never drop below 10 for a solved round). Solve a round in two clicks or fewer to build a streak, shown as a 🔥 multiplier.

After 6 rounds you'll see a shift summary with a total score, a rank (Trainee Operator, Field Operator, Senior Operator, or Shift-in-Charge), and badges for a clean shift (no dangerous excursions), minimum moves, a streak of 3+, and zero near-misses.

The scenario pool currently covers 22 different upsets across reactors, filtration, centrifuges, pumps, heat exchangers, drying, crystallization, scrubbing, and plant utilities (nitrogen, chilled water, instrument air, dust collection) — each shift draws 6 at random, so replaying gives different rounds.
