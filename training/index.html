Plant training for your team

Practical sessions for operators, supervisors, engineers and plant heads in chemical and pharma plants. Built from real plant work: live demos, short videos, quizzes and your own plant's examples, not slides read aloud.

Ask about a session

Topics

Each topic can run as a 2-hour online session or a half-day on site, and can be combined into a full day.

🔥

Static electricity and solvent fires

How charge builds up, why a spark you can’t feel ignites solvent, earthing and bonding, inerting, and powder charging. With a live spark-energy demo and the Barton Solvents case.

Operators · supervisors · engineers
🌡️

Runaway reactions and safe dosing

Heat of reaction, accumulation, MTSR, Stoessel classes, and reading DSC, ARC and RC1 data, ending with the Runaway! game.

Engineers · chemists · plant heads
🔍

HAZOP made practical

Nodes, guidewords, writing a useful row, batch HAZOP and closing actions, with a worked HAZOP on one of your own processes.

Engineers · supervisors · EHS
📋

Scale-up and technology transfer

What changes from lab to plant: heat transfer, mixing, crystallisation, filtration, drying and time, with a transfer checklist your team can reuse.

R&D · tech transfer · production
♨️

Utilities and energy saving

Steam traps and condensate, cooling towers, chillers and VAM, compressed air leaks and nitrogen use, with a walk-down of your own utilities.

Utility and maintenance teams · engineers
⚙️

Pumps, seals and vacuum for operators

Where a pump likes to run, why seals fail, cavitation, and why vacuum “won’t come”, taught with symptoms operators see every day.

Operators · maintenance
