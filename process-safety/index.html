🔥 Case studies: lessons learned the hard way

What happened, why it happened, and what it means for your plant.

1984 · India

Bhopal

A catastrophic methyl isocyanate release from a storage tank at a pesticide plant.

Safeguards out of service · inventory · siting
1974 · UK

Flixborough

A temporary bypass pipe failed and released hot cyclohexane: vapour cloud explosion.

Management of change
2007 · USA

T2 Laboratories

Cooling failed during a batch, the reaction ran away and the reactor exploded.

Reactive hazards · cooling · relief
2005 · UK

Buncefield

A petrol tank was overfilled because both level protections failed: a huge vapour cloud explosion.

Overfill protection · containment
2020 · India

Vizag (LG Polymers)

Styrene in a storage tank heated up and polymerised during the lockdown, releasing vapour over the town.

Reactive storage · restart after shutdown
