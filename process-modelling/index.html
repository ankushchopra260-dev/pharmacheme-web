Process modelling for scale-up

Simple models answer most lab-to-plant questions before the first plant batch: how long the heat-up takes, whether the dosing is safe, how fast the reaction runs at plant temperature, what agitator speed suspends the solids, and how much solvent a swap needs. These five tools run in your browser, free, with your own data, and every result downloads to Excel.

Heating and Cooling Time Simulator

How long a jacketed batch takes to heat or cool, with the temperature curve, jacket duty and steam or TR.

Mixing and Mass Transfer Scale-up

Solvent Swap Simulator

Put-and-take against constant-volume distillation: solvent used, distillate, time and the end temperature.

Open →

A typical workflow

  1. Fit the kinetics from two or three lab runs at different temperatures.
  2. Simulate the dosing at plant scale with the fitted k and Ea, the heat of reaction from RC1 and the plant jacket. Check accumulation, MTSR and the cooling margin.
  3. Check the heat-up and cool-down steps so the batch record times are realistic.
  4. Check the mixing: solids suspension for slurries, kLa for gas reactions, and the process-side heat transfer coefficient for the jacket.
  5. Plan the solvent swap and compare put-and-take with constant-volume distillation.
