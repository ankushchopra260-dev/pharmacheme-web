P&ID Drawing Tool

Pick a symbol, click to place it, and press Connect to join two items.

How to draw a P&ID

  1. Start. Pick a blank sheet, or a ready setup: cooling water distribution, cooling tower loop, reactor, centrifuge and dryer, distillation column, steam and condensate, or a pump transfer.
  2. Place equipment. Click a symbol on the left, then click the sheet. The panel holds every item from the Equipment guide; type in the search box to find one.
  3. Connect. Press Connect, click where the line starts, then where it goes. Click a line to branch off it with a tee, or empty space to add a bend point.
  4. Add valves, pumps and instruments. Click the symbol, then click the line (or the vessel for an instrument). Drag a valve along its line to move it.
  5. Edit. Click anything to change its tag, name, line service or size. Download the drawing as an image when you are done.

Your sheet is kept in this browser, so you can close the page and come back to it. Need a line size? Use the Line Sizing calculator.
