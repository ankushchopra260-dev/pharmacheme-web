What you cover, module by module

Each module builds on the one before it, and ends with something you have calculated yourself.

  1. Module1

    Balances and units

    From kilograms to moles and back to yield, excess reagent, wet cake and loss on drying, and the unit traps that cost money: vacuum versus absolute pressure, kcal versus kW, tonnes of refrigeration.

    You do: a batch material balance with yield, wet cake and water formed.

    Uses: material balance, reading a P&ID, P&ID tool

  2. Module2

    Fluid flow: line sizing, pressure drop and pumps

    Velocity limits, Reynolds number, friction and fittings, pump head and power, NPSH and cavitation.

    You do: size a transfer line and pump, and check NPSH.

    Uses: pressure drop, pump power, the fluid-flow 3D book

  3. Module3

    Heat transfer: jackets and batch heating time

    LMTD and why counter-current wins, overall U, steam use, and how long a batch really takes to heat with steam and cool with cooling water.

    You do: a heat-up and cool-down time for a jacketed reactor.

    Uses: LMTD, overall U, heat load

  4. Module4

    Separations: solvent recovery and filtration

    Boiling under vacuum with the Antoine equation, condenser load and approach, solvent recovery time, and why the last of the filtrate takes the longest: the steps that often set a batch’s cycle.

    You do: plan a solvent recovery: vacuum, temperature, condenser and time.

    Uses: batch distillation simulator, solvent recovery, 138 solvents

  5. Module5

    Reactors and scale-up: mixing, dosing and exotherms

    Impeller power and mixing scale-up from lab to plant, semi-batch dosing and accumulation, the adiabatic rise and the MTSR: what happens when cooling fails on an exothermic batch.

    You do: a safe dosing time and a mixing scale-up from lab to plant.

    Uses: agitator simulator, mixing scale-up, Runaway! game

  6. Module6

    Process safety: HAZOP and inerting

    Taking one node of a reactor through a HAZOP, deviation by deviation, and inerting by pressure or vacuum purge against the limiting oxygen concentration.

    You do: inert a reactor before a flammable charge: cycles, oxygen and nitrogen for both methods.

    Uses: HAZOP made practical, relief sizing, process safety lessons

  7. Module7

    Debottlenecking and cost per kg

    Equipment occupancy, staggered batches and the bottleneck step, and turning output into a cost per kilogram with fixed and variable cost.

    You do: find the bottleneck in a batch and cost the fix.

    Uses: cycle time, OEE, cost per kg

  8. Module8

    Capstone: one batch, end to end

    You take one generic batch and work it through: the balance, the transfer line and pump, the solvent recovery and the monthly output the bottleneck allows. Then the final test.

    You do: the capstone calculation, checked against the worked solution, then a 24-question final test. Pass it for the certificate.
