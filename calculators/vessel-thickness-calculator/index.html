Vessel Thickness and MAWP

Minimum shell and head thickness for internal pressure, the MAWP of a chosen plate thickness and the hydrotest pressure, to ASME Section VIII Division 1.

The Code formulas

Cylindrical shell (UG-27): t = PR / (SE − 0.6P)
2:1 ellipsoidal head (UG-32): t = PD / (2SE − 0.2P)
Torispherical (F&D) head: t = 0.885PL / (SE − 0.1P)
Hemispherical head: t = PR / (2SE − 0.2P) P design pressure, S allowable stress at design temperature (ASME II Part D), E joint efficiency, R and D inside radius and diameter, L crown radius, all in the corroded condition.

Reactors and receivers in pharma plants usually run under vacuum too, and jacketed vessels see the jacket pressure on the outside. External pressure (UG-28) often decides the shell thickness of such vessels and is not covered here. Use this calculator to screen designs and check vendor drawings; the design itself must be done to the applicable Code edition by a competent engineer.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate the shell thickness of a pressure vessel?

Under ASME VIII Div. 1 UG-27, t = PR/(SE − 0.6P) for circumferential stress, with R the inside radius in the corroded condition, then add the corrosion allowance and forming and mill tolerances.
