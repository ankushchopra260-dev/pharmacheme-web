Vessel volume and level

A reactor is a cylinder with two dished ends. Most Indian and European reactors use torispherical (Klöpper, DIN 28011) heads; ASME vessels often use 2:1 elliptical heads.

HeadDepthVolumeInside area
Torispherical (Klöpper)0.1935 D0.0999 D³≈ 0.90 D²
2:1 elliptical0.25 D0.1309 D³≈ 1.084 D²

For a 1.6 m vessel with 1.8 m straight side and Klöpper heads: each head holds about 409 litres and is 310 mm deep; the shell holds about 201 litres per 10 cm; the total is about 4,437 litres. At a level of 1.5 m from the bottom the batch is about 2,803 litres, and 3,000 litres sits at about 1.60 m. Inside a dished end the level-volume relation is not linear; the calculator uses an ellipsoid approximation, within about 2 % for Klöpper heads.

Batch size feasibility

A batch has to fit at every step: above the minimum stirrable volume (agitator and temperature probe covered) and below the maximum working fill (usually 75–85 % of gross, lower for foaming or gassing steps). Enter the volume at each step. If one step overfills and another is too small, the calculator gives the range of scale factors that fits them all, or tells you none does.

Overall U

1/U = 1/hprocess + twall/kwall + tglass/kglass + Rfouling + 1/hjacket

With 800 and 2,000 W/m²K film coefficients, a 12 mm steel wall, 1.5 mm of glass and 0.0004 m²K/W fouling, U ≈ 257 W/m²K, and the glass is the biggest single resistance (39 %). Improve the largest share first. For the wetted jacket area and heat-up time, use the heat load calculator; for condenser area, the solvent recovery calculator.

Refrigeration to cool a batch

Average load = m cp (T1 − T2) / t    Peak = average × (T1 − Tb) ln[(T1 − Tb)/(T2 − Tb)] / (T1 − T2)

Cooling 4,000 kg (cp 2.0) from 40 to 5 °C in 3 hours with −10 °C brine averages about 10 TR but peaks at about 16 TR at the start. Size the chiller and brine pump for the peak. 1 TR = 3.517 kW.

Reagent addition time from RC1 data

Shortest dosing time = Qreaction × (1 − accumulation) / (usable cooling)    MTSR = Tprocess + accumulation × ΔTad

3,000 kg at 260 kJ/kg is 780 MJ. With 15 % accumulation and 70 % of a 95 kW jacket in use, the dosing must take at least about 2.8 hours; the MTSR is about 82 °C, below toluene's 111 °C. The accumulation itself depends on the dosing time, so confirm with an RC1 run at the chosen rate. Background: semi-batch dosing and accumulation.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate the volume of a torispherical dished end?

For a Klöpper head, V ≈ 0.0999 D³ and depth ≈ 0.1935 D: about 409 litres and 310 mm for a 1.6 m vessel.

How do you calculate the TR needed to cool a batch?

Average kW = m cp ΔT / time, divided by 3.517 for TR; size for the peak at the start of cooling, which is often 1.5–2 times the average.

How do you find the minimum dosing time?

Heat released during dosing divided by the cooling you are willing to use: about 2.8 hours in the example.
