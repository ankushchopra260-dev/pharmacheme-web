Drying Agent (Molecular Sieve) Requirement

How much molecular sieve or drying salt brings a solvent down to its water specification. Results update as you type, and the Download Excel button gives a sheet with live formulas.

How it works

Absorbent = water to remove / working capacity × safety factor

Drying 2,000 L of toluene (870 kg/m³) from 2,000 to 200 ppm removes about 3.1 kg of water. At a 10 % working capacity and a 1.3 factor, that is about 41 kg of 3A molecular sieve. Use 3A for methanol and ethanol, because 4A also adsorbs methanol. Confirm by Karl Fischer before and after a small trial.

Frequently asked questions

How much molecular sieve do I need to dry a solvent?
