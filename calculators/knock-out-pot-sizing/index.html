Knock-out Pot and Vertical Separator Sizing

Diameter of a vertical knock-out pot or gas–liquid separator from the Souders–Brown velocity, with GPSA K values and the liquid hold-up height.

How a knock-out pot is sized

Droplets settle out of the gas if the upward gas velocity is below the Souders–Brown limit:

vmax = K √((ρL − ρG) / ρG),   D = √(4 Qactual / (π v))

The GPSA Engineering Data Book gives K = 0.107 m/s for vertical drums with a horizontal wire-mesh pad up to 7 barg, falling with pressure, and suggests about half for drums without a mesh pad. In pharma plants knock-out pots protect vacuum pumps, scrubbers and vent headers from liquid carry-over; a pot under vacuum handles a large actual gas volume, so check the nozzles as well as the shell.

Frequently asked questions

What K value should I use for a knock-out pot?

GPSA gives K = 0.107 m/s for vertical separators with a horizontal mesh pad up to 7 barg, reduced by 0.003 m/s for every 7 bar above that, and about half of these values without a mesh pad.
