Batch Size Feasibility Calculator

Check that every step of a batch fits between the minimum stirrable volume and the maximum working fill, and find the scale range that works. Results update as you type, and the Download Excel button gives a sheet with live formulas.

How it works

A batch has to fit at every step: above the minimum stirrable volume (agitator and temperature probe covered) and below the maximum working fill (usually 75–85 % of gross, lower for foaming or gassing steps). Enter the volume at each step. If one step overfills and another is too small, the calculator gives the range of scale factors that fits them all, or tells you none does.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum working volume of a reactor?

Usually 75 to 85 % of the gross volume, lower for foaming or gassing steps. The minimum stirrable volume is the level that covers the agitator and the temperature probe.
