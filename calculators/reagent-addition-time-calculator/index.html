Reagent Addition Time from RC1 Data

The shortest safe dosing time for an exothermic semi-batch reaction from RC1 data and your plant cooling, and the MTSR. Results update as you type, and the Download Excel button gives a sheet with live formulas.

How it works

Shortest dosing time = Qreaction × (1 − accumulation) / (usable cooling)    MTSR = Tprocess + accumulation × ΔTad

3,000 kg at 62 kcal/kg is 1,86,000 kcal. With 15 % accumulation and 70 % of an 81,000 kcal/h jacket in use, the dosing must take at least about 2.8 hours; the MTSR is about 82 °C, below toluene's 111 °C. The accumulation itself depends on the dosing time, so confirm with an RC1 run at the chosen rate. Background: semi-batch dosing and accumulation.

Frequently asked questions

How do you find the minimum dosing time for an exothermic reaction?

Divide the heat released during dosing (total reaction heat x (1 - accumulation)) by the part of the jacket cooling capacity you are willing to use. With 1,86,000 kcal, 15 % accumulation and 56,700 kcal/h usable cooling, the dosing must take at least about 2.8 hours.
