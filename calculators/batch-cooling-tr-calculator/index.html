Refrigeration (TR) to Cool a Batch

Average and peak refrigeration to cool a batch in a given time, the brine flow and the UA the jacket must give. Results update as you type, and the Download Excel button gives a sheet with live formulas.

How it works

Average load = m cp (T1 − T2) / t    Peak = average × (T1 − Tb) ln[(T1 − Tb)/(T2 − Tb)] / (T1 − T2)

Cooling 4,000 kg (cp 0.48 kcal/kg·°C) from 40 to 5 °C in 3 hours with −10 °C brine averages about 10 TR but peaks at about 16 TR at the start. Size the chiller and brine pump for the peak. 1 TR = 3,024 kcal/h.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate the TR needed to cool a batch?

Average load (kcal/h) = mass x cp (kcal/kg C) x temperature drop / cooling time (h), divided by 3,024 kcal/h per TR. With a fixed jacket the load is highest at the start, so size the chiller for the peak: average x (T1 - Tbrine) x ln((T1 - Tbrine)/(T2 - Tbrine)) / (T1 - T2).
