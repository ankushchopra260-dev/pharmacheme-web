Materials & Corrosion Compatibility

Pick a chemical, see a compatibility rating across 20 common construction materials — SS316, Mild Steel, Hastelloy C276, Titanium, PTFE, PEEK, common plastics, and elastomers.

How This Works

Ratings are compiled from published manufacturer compatibility references, covering 45 common process chemicals against 20 materials of construction. This is a starting reference, not a substitute for a vendor datasheet or an in-house trial — always confirm against your specific concentration, temperature, and duty before finalizing a material of construction, and treat chloride-bearing streams as needing a separate pitting/stress-corrosion-cracking review even where stainless is rated Good.
