What's new

New calculators and changes to existing ones, newest first. Every calculator lists its method and references, and new ones save to a file and print a PDF with your project, tag and engineer.

27 October 2026

  • New game: Plant Rally: drive round a chemical plant, stay safe around hazards, and answer engineering questions that use the same formulas as the calculators, each with a worked solution.

26 October 2026

  • New: Batch distillation simulator: stages, reflux and cuts with non-ideal VLE (modified UNIFAC).
  • New: Relief valve sizing to API 520/521: gas, vapour, steam, liquid, fire case for wetted and gas-filled vessels, thermal relief, API 526 orifices.
  • New: Control valve Cv for liquid, gas and steam with choked-flow checks, and restriction orifice sizing.
  • New: Steam tables from IAPWS-IF97 in kcal/kg, flash steam and desuperheater.
  • New: Pipe pressure drop with fittings (3-K method) and pump NPSH available.
  • New: Jacketed vessel heat transfer, LMTD and F correction and insulation heat loss.
  • New: Knock-out pot sizing, vessel thickness and MAWP and tank filling and breathing losses.
  • New: Unit converter with gauge, absolute and vacuum pressure, and gas flow at normal, standard and actual conditions.
  • New: Azeotrope calculator and table: 216 measured azeotropes, estimates at any pressure with T–x–y and x–y diagrams; azeotropes listed on 93 solvent pages.
  • Improved: Mixing scale-up: enter your own power number for any impeller.
  • For all new calculators: worked example, save to file and open file, link that carries your inputs, and a PDF with project, tag and engineer.
  • Improved: every older calculator’s Download PDF can now carry project, tag and engineer in the header.
  • Fixed: on phones, scrolling a comparison table no longer jumps back to case 1 (only a clear sideways swipe changes case), and Download PDF now shows an “Open PDF” button in case the phone blocks the download.

21 October 2026

  • New: Digitalization and AI article series: predictive maintenance, soft sensors, digital twins, eBMR, OEE, OT cybersecurity, KPI dashboard and a business-case calculator.
