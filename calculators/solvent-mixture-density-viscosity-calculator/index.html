How it works

70 % toluene with 30 % methanol by mass: about 843 kg/m³ and 0.55 cP. These ideal rules are good for similar solvents. Water with alcohols, acids or DMF is far from ideal: methanol-water viscosity peaks above both pure liquids, and the volume shrinks on mixing, so measure for design.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate the density of a solvent mixture?

For an ideal mixture, 1/density = sum of mass fraction / density of each component. Viscosity follows ln(mu) = sum of mole fraction x ln(mu i). 70 % toluene with 30 % methanol by mass is about 843 kg/m3 and 0.55 cP.
