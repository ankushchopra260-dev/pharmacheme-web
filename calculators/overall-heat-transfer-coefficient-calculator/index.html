Overall Heat Transfer Coefficient (U) Calculator

The overall U of a jacketed vessel from its film coefficients, wall, glass lining and fouling, with the share of each resistance. Results update as you type, and the Download Excel button gives a sheet with live formulas.

How it works

1/U = 1/hprocess + twall/kwall + tglass/kglass + Rfouling + 1/hjacket

With 800 and 2,000 W/m²K film coefficients, a 12 mm steel wall, 1.5 mm of glass and 0.0004 m²K/W fouling, U ≈ 257 W/m²K, and the glass is the biggest single resistance (39 %). Improve the largest share first. For the wetted jacket area and heat-up time, use the heat load calculator; for condenser area, the solvent recovery calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate the overall heat transfer coefficient?

1/U = 1/h process + wall thickness/wall conductivity + glass thickness/glass conductivity + fouling + 1/h jacket. With 800 and 2,000 W/m2K films, a 12 mm steel wall, 1.5 mm glass and 0.0004 m2K/W fouling, U is about 257 W/m2K.
