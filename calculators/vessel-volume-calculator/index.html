Vessel Volume and Level Calculator

Total volume of a dished-end reactor, the liquid volume at any level, and the level for a given volume. Results update as you type, and the Download Excel button gives a sheet with live formulas.

How it works

A reactor is a cylinder with two dished ends. Most Indian and European reactors use torispherical (Klöpper, DIN 28011) heads; ASME vessels often use 2:1 elliptical heads.

HeadDepthVolumeInside area
Torispherical (Klöpper)0.1935 D0.0999 D³≈ 0.90 D²
2:1 elliptical0.25 D0.1309 D³≈ 1.084 D²

For a 1.6 m vessel with 1.8 m straight side and Klöpper heads: each head holds about 409 litres and is 310 mm deep; the shell holds about 201 litres per 10 cm; the total is about 4,437 litres. At a level of 1.5 m from the bottom the batch is about 2,803 litres, and 3,000 litres sits at about 1.60 m. Inside a dished end the level-volume relation is not linear; the calculator uses an ellipsoid approximation, within about 2 % for Klöpper heads.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate the volume of a torispherical dished end?

For a Kloepper (DIN 28011) torispherical head the inside volume is about 0.0999 x D cubed and the depth about 0.1935 x D. For a 1.6 m vessel that is about 409 litres per head, 310 mm deep. A 2:1 elliptical head holds 0.1309 x D cubed.
