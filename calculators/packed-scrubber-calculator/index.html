How it works

D = √(4Q/πu)    NTU = ln[1/(1 − η)]    Packed height = NTU × HTU

500 Nm³/h at 40 °C and a design velocity of 1.2 m/s gives a column of about 410 mm. 99 % removal is 4.6 transfer units; with HTU = 0.5 m that is 2.3 m of packing, 2.8 m with margin. A 5 kg/h HCl peak uses about 5.5 kg/h of NaOH, 55 kg/h of 10 % solution. Check the velocity against the packing supplier's flooding chart. Background: scrubber design for process vents.

Frequently asked questions

How do you size a packed scrubber?

Diameter = square root of (4 x gas flow / (pi x design velocity)). Packed height = number of transfer units x HTU, where NTU = ln(1/(1 - removal efficiency)) for a fast reaction. 99 % removal is 4.6 transfer units.
