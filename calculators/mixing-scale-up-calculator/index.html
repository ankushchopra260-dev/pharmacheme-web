Mixing / Scale-up Calculator

Reactor geometry, agitation Reynolds number, impeller tip speed, power draw, power-per-volume (P/V), and torque — with side-by-side case comparison across plants or scales.

How to Use

Enter vessel and impeller geometry, RPM, and fluid properties for each case you want to compare. The tool computes Reynolds number, tip speed, power number-based draw, P/V, and torque, laid out as a comparison table so you can judge whether a lab or pilot condition is being scaled consistently to plant scale.

Engineering Notes & Assumptions

Constant P/V, constant tip speed, and constant Reynolds number are three different (and mutually exclusive) scale-up rules — which one is appropriate depends on whether your process is shear-sensitive, mass-transfer-limited, or blend-time-limited. This tool reports the numbers; picking the right scale-up criterion for your chemistry is still an engineering judgment call.
