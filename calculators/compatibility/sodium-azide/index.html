Sodium azide: dangerous mixtures

Reactive group: Azide · CAS 26628-22-8.

Forms explosive heavy-metal azides with copper, lead and brass (drains, fittings).

  • Dangerous with acid halide / water-reactive chloride · Heat, Explosion
    Violent reaction; acyl azides and some hydrazides are explosive.
    For example: Phosphorus Trichloride (PCl3), Phosphorus Pentachloride, Phosphorus Oxychloride (POCl3), Thionyl Chloride, Sulfuryl Chloride, Oxalyl Chloride, Acetyl Chloride, Benzoyl Chloride and 4 more.
  • Dangerous with strong acid, concentrated sulphuric acid, nitric acid (oxidising acid) and organic acid · Toxic gas: HN₃, Explosion
    Forms hydrazoic acid: very toxic, volatile and explosive.
    For example: Acetic Acid, Glacial, Formic Acid, Propionic Acid, Trifluoroacetic Acid (TFA), Methanesulfonic Acid (MSA), p-Toluenesulfonic Acid (PTSA), Hydrochloric Acid (37%), Sulfuric Acid (<10%) and 5 more.
  • Dangerous with chlorinated solvent · Explosion
    Sodium azide with DCM or chloroform forms di- and triazidomethane, which are explosive.
    For example: Methylene Chloride, Chloroform, Carbon Tetrachloride, 1,2-Dichloroethane (EDC), Trichloroethylene, Tetrachloroethylene, Chlorobenzene, o-Dichlorobenzene and 1 more.
  • Dangerous with carbon disulfide · Explosion
    Forms explosive azidodithiocarbonate.
    For example: Carbon Disulfide.
