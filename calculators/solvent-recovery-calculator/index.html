Solvent Recovery Calculator

Batch vacuum distillation design, drawing on an embedded ~4,979-compound solvent database with Antoine coefficients.

What's Inside

This tool bundles four related sub-calculators, reached from the panel above:

  • Boiling Point at Vacuum — solves the Antoine equation, Tb = B / (A − log₁₀P) − C, for the boiling temperature at your applied vacuum.
  • Heat Load & Recovery Time — sizing for how much heat duty and time a batch recovery will take. Also reachable directly at the Heat Load & Recovery Calculator.
  • Condenser Sizing — duty and area estimate for the recovery condenser.
  • Vacuum Pump Capacity — required pump capacity for the vacuum level and vapor load.

Engineering Notes & Assumptions

Antoine coefficients are compound-specific and only valid over the temperature/pressure range they were fitted to — very high vacuum or near-critical conditions can fall outside that range. Always sanity-check the returned boiling point against a known reference point for the solvent before using it for equipment sizing.
