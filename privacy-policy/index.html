Privacy Policy

PharmaChemE (pharmacheme.in) is run by Ankush Chopra. This page explains what the site collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can ask us to show or delete it. Last updated 6 November 2026.

We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising on this site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google sets cookies and receives your page views, approximate location (country and city level), and device and browser details. We do not use it to identify you by name. The site does not show a cookie banner at present. You can opt out with Google’s Analytics Opt-out Add-on, by blocking cookies in your browser, or by using a browser with tracking protection. Google’s own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data kept only in your browser

The calculators, the URS builder, the molecule and reaction tools and the games run in your browser. What you enter is not sent to us. Some tools save your work or settings in your browser’s local storage so that it is there when you return (for example the URS builder, reaction and structure sketches, and install or guide prompts you have dismissed). The site also uses a service worker to keep copies of pages and files so that it can open offline. You can clear all of this at any time in your browser settings. Clearing it deletes that saved work.

Google sign-in, paid items and Exclusive content

Paid and Exclusive items use Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication, so we can tell which Google account should have access. When you sign in and ask for an item, we store in Google Firestore:

  • your Google account email address and display name;
  • your purchase request: the item, its title and price, and the date and time;
  • the UPI transaction reference (UTR) you type in after paying, and the time you confirmed it;
  • your access record, which we set after we have confirmed your payment;
  • your progress in items you have opened, so you can pick up where you stopped.

We use this to give you access, to check payments, to answer questions about your purchase and to keep our records. Payment itself is made by UPI outside this site. We do not receive or store your bank, card or UPI credentials. When you make a request or confirm a payment, the site also sends the same details (email, name, item, price, UTR and the page you were on) to a Google Apps Script notification address that tells us a request has arrived. Free items do not need you to sign in.

Firebase and Google Sign-In are Google services. Their privacy information is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Enquiry and training forms

The home page enquiry form, the Custom Solutions form and the Training request form send what you type to us through Formspree. Depending on the form that can include your name, company, email, phone number, plant location and type, topic, group size, your message, and any file you attach. We use it only to reply to you and to scope the work you asked about. Formspree’s policy is at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Online tests run for colleges

Some pages run timed tests for colleges that have asked us to host them. These pages are not linked from the main site. When you take such a test, your name, roll number, college, access code, your answers and the time taken are sent to a Google Apps Script that records them in a Google Sheet, so that results can be given to the college. Your progress inside a test is held in your browser’s session storage until you submit.

Embedded videos

Where a page shows a video from the PharmaChemE YouTube channel, it first shows only a thumbnail loaded from YouTube. The YouTube player and its cookies load only after you press play. YouTube’s privacy information is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Other services the pages load

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some tools load code libraries from public content delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg and Google’s own servers). Loading these files means those providers can see your IP address and browser details, as with any website.

How long we keep data

Records in our database are set to delete themselves after a fixed period:

  • Purchase requests that were never paid: 90 days.
  • Paid purchase records (email, name, item, price, UTR): 8 years from payment, because we need them for accounting and tax.
  • Learning progress (which items you opened or finished): 2 years after your last activity.
  • Access record (the switch that opens paid items for your email): kept while your access is valid, then removed on request.

Enquiry and test submissions are kept for as long as needed to respond, and no longer than 2 years. Analytics data is kept by Google under its own settings. If you want something deleted sooner, ask us (see below) and we will delete it unless we need to keep it for a legal or accounting reason.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your sign-in access by asking us to remove your access record. Email pharmacheme.app@gmail.com from the address you used and tell us what you want. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can. You can also remove PharmaChemE’s access to your Google account from your Google account settings. This page is a plain explanation of our practice and is not legal advice. If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 may give you further rights, and you can use the contact above to exercise them.

Children

The site is written for students and working engineers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has sent us personal data, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the site’s data practices change, and change the date at the top. Please check it from time to time.
