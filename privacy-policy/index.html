Privacy Policy

PharmaChemE is a static website. It does not require an account for the calculators, molecule tool, or games — anything you enter into those runs locally in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

This site uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic — which pages are visited, roughly how many people visit, and general information like device type and country. This does not identify you personally. You can opt out using a browser extension like Google's Analytics Opt-out Add-on, or by using a browser with tracking protection enabled.

The Custom Solutions enquiry form sends what you type (name, email, company, and your message) to our email via a third-party form service (Formspree), solely to respond to your enquiry. It is not used for any other purpose.

If a Google Sign-In feature is added to this site, it will only be used to let you identify yourself to features that need it (such as saving a preference) — it will not be used to track your activity elsewhere, and this policy will be updated with specifics before that feature goes live.
