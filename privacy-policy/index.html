Privacy Policy

PharmaChemE is a static website. As of this version, it does not use analytics, does not require an account, and does not store any data you enter into the calculators, molecule tool, or games — all of that runs locally in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

The Custom Solutions enquiry form is currently frontend-only and not yet connected to any backend, so information typed into it is not transmitted or stored anywhere at this time. This policy will be updated before that form, any analytics, or any account/login feature is switched on.
