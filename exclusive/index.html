Questions people ask

How do I pay?

Open the item you want, sign in with Google and tap Buy now. Scan the QR with any UPI app (the amount fills in by itself), or on a phone tap to open your UPI app. Then enter the 12-digit UTR from your UPI app. We check the payment and switch the item on for your Google email, usually within a few hours, and email you when it opens.

What if something goes wrong?

Write to pharmacheme.app@gmail.com with your UTR. If we cannot give you access, we refund the full amount to the same UPI account.

Can I buy a whole learning path at once?

Yes. Sign in, find the path above and tap Buy the rest of this path. Pay the total in one UPI payment and enter the UTR; we switch every item on together.

How do I get the certificate?

Finish every exclusive item in a path: answer the quick checks on each page of a 3D book, mark articles and notes as finished, and use each calculator or template once. The certificate then appears on the path, ready to print or save as PDF.

Do the 3D books work on a phone?

Yes. Drag with one finger to turn the model. A recent phone or any laptop runs them smoothly.

How long do I keep access?

Books, articles, notes, Pro calculators, templates and Excel versions stay open for your Google account. The GATE test series is open until 31 March 2027.

Are the free calculators going away?

No. Every free calculator stays free. Where a calculator has an Excel version with live formulas, you will find it offered on that calculator's page.
