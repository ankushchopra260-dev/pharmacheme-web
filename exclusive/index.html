Questions people ask

How do I pay?

Open the item you want, sign in with Google and tap Request access. We email you our UPI ID. Pay from any UPI app and reply with the transaction reference. We switch the item on for your Google email, usually within a day.

Can I buy a whole learning path at once?

Yes. Sign in, find the path above and tap Request the rest of this path. We send one UPI request for the total and switch every item on together.

How do I get the certificate?

Finish every paid step of a path: answer the quick checks on each page of a 3D book, mark articles and notes as finished, and use each calculator or template once. The certificate then appears on the path, ready to print or save as PDF.

Do the 3D books work on a phone?

Yes. Drag with one finger to turn the model. A recent phone or any laptop runs them smoothly.

How long do I keep access?

Books, articles, notes, Pro calculators, templates and Excel versions stay open for your Google account. The GATE test series is open until 31 March 2027.

Are the free calculators going away?

No. Every free calculator stays free. Where a calculator has an Excel version with live formulas, you will find it offered on that calculator's page.
