Sulfonamide formation from a sulfonyl chloride

An amine attacks the sulfur of a sulfonyl chloride and chloride leaves, giving a sulfonamide. A base takes up the HCl. Sulfonamides are found in many drugs, from antibacterials to diuretics, and the reaction is fast, clean and exothermic.

Background and plant practice: Common chemical reactions in API and agrochemical plants.

Gas evolvedNone with base present
Moisture sensitivityHigh
HeatStrongly exothermic
TypeNitrogen chemistry

Benzenesulfonyl chloride + Amine (methylamine shown) + Triethylamine → Sulfonamide + Triethylamine hydrochloride (DCM, THF or water/base, 0–25 °C)

In the lab

  1. Charge the amine and triethylamine in the solvent; cool to 0–5 °C.
  2. Dose the sulfonyl chloride (solution or melt) slowly. Exothermic; the hydrochloride salt comes out.
  3. Warm to 20 °C and stir until the amine is consumed.
  4. Wash with dilute acid and water; separate the layers.
  5. Sulfonamide in the organic layer.

Mechanism

  1. The amine nitrogen attacks sulfur and chloride leaves (substitution at sulfur).
  2. Triethylamine removes the proton from nitrogen.
  3. Products: the sulfonamide and triethylamine hydrochloride.

What to take care of

  • Sulfonyl chlorides react with water to give the sulfonic acid and HCl; keep reagents dry until the reaction is complete.
  • The reaction is fast and exothermic; dose with cooling.
  • Sulfonyl chlorides are corrosive and lachrymatory; use closed transfer.
  • Residual sulfonyl chloride and sulfonate esters (if alcohols are present) can be genotoxic; quench excess before work-up.

Gas evolution

No gas is formed in the main reaction (none with base present).

By-products and how to control them

By-productCause and control
Sulfonic acidHydrolysis of the sulfonyl chloride by water. Dry solvent and amine.
Bis-sulfonylation (N,N-disulfonyl)Primary amines with excess sulfonyl chloride and strong base. Use about 1.0–1.05 eq.
Sulfonate esterAlcohol solvent or hydroxyl groups react. Avoid alcohol solvents.

Moisture sensitivity: High

Water consumes the sulfonyl chloride, forming sulfonic acid and HCl and lowering the yield. Aqueous-base versions work only because the amine reacts faster than water.

Heat of reaction

Sulfonylation plus neutralisation of HCl release significant heat; run dosing-controlled at low temperature.

Plant tips

  • Assay the sulfonyl chloride lot; it hydrolyses in storage.
  • Keep the dosing line dry and flush it after use.
  • Check genotoxic impurity limits for sulfonyl chloride and esters.
